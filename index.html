Publications

BlinkMimic: Training a Humanoid Motion Tracker in Minutes on a Single GPU
Fuyi Chen
arXiv, 2026
arXiv | code (300+ stars) | comparison video

One policy tracks all 40 LAFAN1 motion clips on a simulated Unitree G1 with zero failures after 3.4 minutes of training on one RTX 4090. The policy is trained by back-propagating through a differentiable world model learned from parallel MuJoCo Warp simulation.

Same Path, Less Time: Training-Free Fast Execution of Vision-Language-Action Models under Inference Latency
Fuyi Chen
arXiv, 2026
arXiv

VLA policies pause during inference and replay the slow pace of human demonstrations. PTS is a training-free execution layer that keeps where the arm moves but changes how fast it moves, raising task throughput on LIBERO by 33% (π0.5) and 104% (SmolVLA) with equal or higher success.

Label-Free Abstract Visual Reasoning on Raven's Progressive Matrices
Fuyi Chen, Jianfeng Ren
In preparation
project page

Learns to solve Raven's Progressive Matrices from the eight given panels alone, with no labels and no answer candidates during training, a stricter setting than prior label-free work. 94.3% on I-RAVEN (+4.6 over the best label-free method, 1.1 below the same network trained with answers) and 97.7% on RAVEN-FAIR.
