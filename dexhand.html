Vision-Based Motion Retargeting from Human Videos to Dexterous Robot Hands

University of Nottingham Ningbo China · Jun 2026 – present

Goal

Teleoperation rigs for dexterous hands are expensive and slow to use. Ordinary videos of human hands are cheap and plentiful. This project turns RGB videos of a human hand into robot-hand motion, as a scalable source of demonstrations for dexterous manipulation.

Pipeline (real2sim)

RGB video→ MediaPipe: 21 hand keypoints→ human hand motion→ dex-retargeting (kinematic optimisation)→ robot joint trajectory
  • Hand tracking. MediaPipe Hands extracts 21 keypoints per frame from a monocular RGB video.
  • Retargeting. dex-retargeting solves a kinematic optimisation that maps the human keypoints to the joint angles of a multi-DoF robot hand, frame by frame.
  • Output. A per-frame joint-angle trajectory that drives the robot hand in simulation.

Results

The pipeline reproduces single-finger, whole-hand and special-gesture motions. In each clip, the left side is the input video with the tracked keypoints and the right side is the retargeted robot hand.
